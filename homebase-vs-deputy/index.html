Start with your staffing model

Homebase Basic is $0 for a single location with at most 10 employees. Essentials is $30 monthly per location. Deputy Lite is $5 monthly per billable user, with a $30 minimum per monthly invoice. Managers and administrators count toward Deputy's billed users.

When Homebase is a sensible shortlist choice

Start with Homebase if you operate a single small venue and need basic shift planning and time capture without a subscription fee. Its paid location-based model can also make staffing growth easier to budget for. Check your required features and POS integration before committing.

When Deputy deserves a closer look

Evaluate Deputy if demand-based staffing, automatic schedule building or more sophisticated labor planning matter more than the lowest subscription price. Those advanced workflows are listed on Core, priced at $6.50 per user per month, rather than Lite.

Before you choose

Count billable managers and workers, confirm the number of physical locations, identify the lowest plan that includes your must-have workflows, and obtain a quote for any payroll or optional tools. Test a real weekly schedule before migrating your staff.

Decision shortcut

For a small restaurant seeking basic scheduling at low cost, Homebase is a practical first candidate. If advanced labor planning is your primary constraint, compare Deputy Core against the Homebase tier that supports your workflow. Do not choose solely by the entry price.
